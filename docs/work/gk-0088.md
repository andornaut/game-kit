## Criteria

- Use shared directional navigation and focused activation within the active foreground surface.
  Preserve native editing, composition, selection, Tab, owned control keys and primary input.
- Route a distinct Escape press to one top surface, giving its visible eligible Skip precedence
  over dismissal. Preserve caller guards, mandatory choices, focus restoration and pause state.
  A consumed press remains contained across surface transitions.
- Use existing action guards for eligible secondary activation and suppress the context menu
  only for consumed gestures. Keep gameplay Use and surface actions caller-owned.
- Qualify nested dialogs, pages and recovery hosts, then provide checked public declarations and
  support each game's owned keyboard-only qualification and adoption.
