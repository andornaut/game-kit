## Criteria

- Qualify a measurable improvement during active play in each current game. Game Tech owns
  consumer source, acceptance and delivery. GameKit owns shared mechanisms supported by evidence.
- Target a 20% lower p95 frame interval in Ada's matched battle benchmark. Record p50, p95,
  p99, long frames, renderer, device and workload alongside the exact before and after revisions.
- Preserve gameplay, visuals and save behavior. Verify gains on matched inputs and settings,
  record physical-device evidence where available, and confirm each normal delivery.
