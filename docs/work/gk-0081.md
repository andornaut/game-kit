## Criteria

- Improve one shared game runtime path with evidence that callers use it during play.
- Compare the before and after on the same representative workload in isolation.
- Preserve public behavior and game-owned policy. Keep any benchmark evidence separate from the production package.

## Outcome

The crumbling terrain step now builds its support lookup and checks durations without allocating
temporary arrays. In 12 warmed, alternating pairs of an isolated two-platform Chibi workload,
median CPU time per 1.8 million ticks fell from 199.26 ms to 158.15 ms. Recorded phase and contact
snapshots matched. The benchmark measures shared terrain code, with a synthetic actor trace.
