## Criteria

- Store and serve group archives compressed. Installs and updates decompress
  and verify them before storing their members. First-install and update wire
  bytes for a grouped build stay near the gzip size of their members, without
  relying on host compression of `application/octet-stream`.
- Keep group reuse across builds, pins, retry, progress, save fences, single
  activation and `createGroupArchiveMemberReader`. Progress reports wire bytes.
- Export an encoder for the group archive format, so games can unit-test their
  reader paths against real archives.
- Check that the kit's grouped pointer validation accepts every pointer the
  kit emits, including tagged and grouped first-install pointers.

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.73.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.73.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

Static `updateGroup` builds emit each group as one deterministic gzip member
at `/.game-kit/group-<sha256>.bin` (`buildGroupUrl`). The pointer group keeps
`byteSize` and `sha256` of the decoded archive and adds `encoding: "gzip"` and
`encodedByteSize`, so unchanged groups keep their digests, devices reuse
groups stored by earlier kits, and the build version does not depend on the
transport. The page reads the exact transport length, decodes within the
archive size, verifies the archive digest and only then stores it, so the
router, group store, pins and activation are unchanged. Progress counts
transport bytes. `createGroupArchiveMemberReader` reads and verifies the
transport. Recipe groups keep the decoded transport.

Pages on 0.72.0 and earlier stage only decoded archives, so the build still
emits them at their earlier paths until installed devices have moved to
0.73.0 (`CLEANUP`). `encodeGroupArchive` from `build/groupArchive` produces
the same archives for tests.

`validateBuildPointer` from `build/buildManifest` rejected every pointer with
`archive`, `groups` or `groupedFirstInstall`. It now accepts every emitted
shape, and the producer builds its pointer through it. `parseBuildPointer`,
the group store, the router and the member reader already accepted tagged and
grouped first-install pointers and now also check the transport fields.

The grouped browser fixture, with compressible assets, sent 88,829 wire bytes
for 219,587 bytes of decoded first-install archives, and 82,590 and 82,582
wire bytes for 213,361 on its two updates.
