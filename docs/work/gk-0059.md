## Criteria

- The crash screen and the startup failure and capability error card take
  their type from the uiTheme text roles and their colours from the uiTheme
  slots, so each game's tokens apply, with defaults when a game sets none.
- Keep their behaviour, copy and Retry rules unchanged.
- Reference: Chibi captures
  `var/handoffs/games/chibis-way-home/tech/cwh-0306-captures-20261125/b/`.

## Outcome

Additive. Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

- `web/crash/dom/crash.css` takes the text roles and colour slots a game sets
  on an ancestor such as the document root, in CSS alone, at the inherited
  `--ui-unit`: `heading` for the title, `body` for the text, `label` for Reload
  and Copy, `supporting` for the dev stack; `textPrimary`, `frame` and
  `cardFace` for the text, panel border and panel face. Copy takes the button
  slots and Reload, now `data-ui-button-role="primary"`, the primary slots;
  focus and press take their slots as fills.
- `mountLoadingFailure`, and with it the `prepareBrowserLaunch` capability
  error, takes `heading`, `body` and `label` for its title, text and Retry;
  `cardFace`, `textPrimary` and `frame` for its panel, text and borders; and
  the primary, focus and pressed button slots for Retry, configured or native.
- Each value falls back to the screen's own: `CrashColours` and the crash
  type, or the card's presentation, else its native system fallback. A page
  that sets none renders as before, also under a `--ui-unit`.
- Unchanged: copy, Retry rules, focus handling, forced colours, the lip,
  shadow, highlight and shade colours, panel geometry and the summary's focus
  outline.
