## Scope

Use the deployed version to decide whether an update needs a download.
Expose whole-build staging as a build-time option. Ada enables it for its
large file inventory. Archie and Chibi continue using delta staging.
For an archive-enabled build, fetch and verify the whole build once.
Keep the archive intact and serve its entries through a validated URL index.
The archive path performs no individual hash checks, downloads or cache copies.

## Criteria

- The producer covers every offline URL, including document, boot files and
  sound alternatives. Preserve the logical URL and response semantics of each
  entry. Reject malformed archives, missing coverage and mismatched identity.
- Verify the archive's exact length and full SHA-256 before publishing its
  staged or active record. A failed or interrupted download leaves the prior
  build usable. Existing installs can transition without a cache or save reset.
- Keep live documents pinned to their build and retain offline playback,
  cold-launch activation, ordinary in-session consent and newer-save fences.
- Verify that the controlling router can serve the archive before an
  archive-only build can activate. Keep delta staging available for builds
  that omit the archive option.
- Compare time to staged readiness, bytes transferred, app responsiveness and
  storage use against the same existing build pair. Include network and offline
  verification on the exact archive and preserve normal publication checks.

## Outcome

`precachePlugin` can emit an archive for whole-build updates alongside
ordinary first-install assets and packs. The page verifies and stores the
archive, and a capable worker serves its indexed entries. Existing
installations and builds without the option keep their current transport.

The Chromium fixture covers staging, consent and saves, offline indexed
responses, and dismissal followed by cold-launch activation. Ada's delivery
receipts track the production option and the comparison with delta staging.
