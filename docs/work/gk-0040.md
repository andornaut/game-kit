## Scope

A timer-held touch can dismiss the top modal and restore its opener before
the finger lifts. Chromium's compatibility mousedown can then land on the
newly exposed view and steal focus from the opener.

## Criteria

- Preserve focus for that gesture's matching touch-derived mousedown.
- Keep the original action single-shot, including when it invalidates the
  gesture while closing the modal.
- Preserve short taps, unrelated mouse and keyboard behavior, and the
  existing release ownership and disposal rules.

## Outcome

Pointer-release bindings retain the focus change across a timer-held action
and match it against the associated trusted touch release. A fresh gesture
or lifecycle reset clears that match. The Chromium regression exercises
both a short tap and a timer-held dismissal, including keyboard navigation
and a subsequent native mouse action.
