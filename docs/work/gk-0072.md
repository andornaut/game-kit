## Criteria

- `place()` takes a per-row height, so rows of different heights share one
  placed list, with tracks, gaps and art-locked parts unchanged in rule.
- A one-action row's face centres in the full row, not inside the list
  padding.
- Lists without per-row heights, and rows without an action, render as
  before.

## Outcome

Additive, apart from the requested one-action centring. A placed list without per-row heights,
and every row without an action, renders as in 0.90.0, which `unprojected.test.ts` asserts by
digests taken from 0.90.0's output: of a placed list with art pixels, gaps and a press dip, and
of the unplaced markup, the list's tracks and a switch row's markup and geometry beside a
one-action row in a padded list.

- Per-row heights: `createFormRows` `place` takes `{ element, height }` beside `gap`, and
  `FormRowsRow.height` sets it in `formRowsGeometry`, in whole units. A row without one is a
  slider's `layout.height` or the layout's `height`. The row's track, padding, icon, switch,
  lane, art-locked sizes and press dip follow its own height.
- One-action centring: the face centres in the whole row box, the list's padding included, the
  odd unit right and below, and must fit the row. `formRow.css` gives a one-action row no
  padding, and a placed row writes `--ui-form-action-top` from the row's top. Faces in lists with
  unequal opposite padding move; the README's 0.91.0 note records it.
- Proved on Archie's grid at 390x844@3 (art pixel 3): Settings as one list (Sound, Music,
  Effects, Motion at 82 and 56, Install and FPS at 48, gap 17) places every row, icon, switch,
  lane, dip and Install's face exactly as its three lists did: the face is 92 units into its 330
  unit row, where centring in the padded content would put it at 91.
