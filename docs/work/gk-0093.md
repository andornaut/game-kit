## Criteria

- Let callers keep the guard active in play, on Start, and during caller-owned Exit/Cancel.
- On confirmed Exit, release the close watcher and unwind only the currently owned fallback history entry before one ordinary platform leave action.
- Preserve other history entries, keyboard Escape handling and existing caller behaviour.
- Verify cancel, confirmed exit and stale or overlapping history updates without game-specific rules.

The callers follow the [shared Back policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/c6547e30091550b39fc50c18a7096a739986b0a1/docs/design/game-policies.md#back-close-and-resume).
