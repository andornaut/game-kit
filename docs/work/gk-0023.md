## Scope

Recover keyboard navigation when a backdrop press leaves document focus outside the active
strict dialog. Preserve caller-owned backdrop behavior and mandatory decisions.

## Criteria

- Tab and Shift+Tab enter the active dialog at the first and last eligible control, respectively.
- Neutral keys cannot activate controls or dismiss a dialog. Legacy isolation and backdrop
  pointer handling retain their behavior.

## Decisions

Reuse the strict dialog's existing focus order and ownership checks.
