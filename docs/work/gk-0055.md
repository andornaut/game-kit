## Criteria

- Backdrop, centred card with dialog roles and ids, width families, a header
  with a centred title and mirrored close clearance, scroll body, footer and
  close button, built on the gk-0054 layout base.
- Reference implementations: Chibi `cardElements.ts` and `ui.css` (K1) and Ada
  ada-0390 item 3.
- Card centred both ways, width families as data with a 16 px screen margin,
  height fitted to content. Full-screen views anchor the close button to the
  view.
- The close press target is never clamped or shifted. The centred title
  reserves the full close target plus a gap symmetrically, with an ink offset
  the game can set.
- The top dialog isolates covered chrome as dimmed and inert, and focus
  returns to the opener.
- The frame accepts a game skin (CSS or 9-slice art) and the game's content
  rect, and adds no padding the game cancels.

## Outcome

- `runtime/ui/modalFrame`: `modalFrameRooms(context)` and `placeModalFrame(context, sizes)`
  place a card at the game's width family, clamped inside a 16 unit margin of the game's content
  rect and centred both ways (odd unit right and below), or a full-screen frame that is the
  content rect. Height follows content up to the room; past it the body scrolls. The close face
  and press target are corner rects placed exactly as given, the press target defaulting to
  `HIT_RULE` about the face. The title reserves the whole target plus the gap on both sides, the
  header reaches the gap (plus the body's press outset) below the target, and the title takes the
  game's ink offset. Body insets, footer room and a projection hook complete it.
- `runtime/web/ui/dom/modalFrame` with `modalFrame.css`: backdrop, dialog card with role, ids,
  `aria-labelledby` and optional `aria-describedby`, a skin layer for a CSS face or nine-slice
  `.ui-frame`, header and title, scroll body, a footer on the gk-0054 action group's footer
  variant and the game's close control, last in focus order, whose box is its face and whose
  `::before` is the unclamped press target. `layout()` measures and places every part;
  `open()` uses the modal layer's dialog isolation with the backdrop as the outside target, an
  optional `modalInput` scope and optional backdrop taps on the game's one dismissal, and returns
  the focus to the opener. All rules have zero specificity; the backdrop dim is
  `--ui-modal-backdrop`.
- Width families stay game data: Ada and Chibi `{ large: 688, medium: 500, small: 400 }`,
  Archie `{ large: 328, medium: 264 }`.
- Not covered: dimming HTML chrome by palette ramp (Chibi's dimmed skin) stays with the game; the
  backdrop dim covers games that dim by a fill. Families alone set the width: `modalWidth`'s
  half-viewport minimum is not applied, and at Archie's 360 width it never binds.
