## Scope

Support a visible, unavailable standard button that skips keyboard navigation and gives
pointer refusal feedback without requiring an announced reason. Adopt the existing shared
gesture binding. Keep the ordinary unavailable state and native disabled behavior.

## Criteria

- A pointer or touch attempt gives the existing refusal feedback once and cannot activate
  the unavailable action.
- The unavailable control is skipped by keyboard navigation and cannot trigger refusal
  from keyboard or assistive activation. Re-enabling restores normal navigation and the
  live action.
- Existing focus, modal and readiness guards and callers retain their behavior.

## Outcome

The standard renderer supports pointer refusal without a reason or a keyboard tab stop. It keeps gesture admission current and restores the original tab-order setting on recovery or disposal. Focused button, hold and modal checks passed.
