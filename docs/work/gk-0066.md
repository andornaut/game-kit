## Criteria

- The pager takes an optional game `refuse(reducedMotion, control)` callback
  that runs for both pointer and key refusals, in place of the kit cue.
- Without it, the kit refusal cue stays the default and behaves as today.
- The rule that a refusal does not restart a playing cue applies to the
  kit cue; a caller cue owns its own replay behaviour.

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.86.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.86.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

Additive. A pager without the new option behaves as before. Consumer adoption follows each
game's normal delivery.

- `runtime/web/ui/dom/pager` `PagerRefusal`: `refusal` takes `{ cue, reducedMotion }` as before
  or `{ refuse, reducedMotion }`, the game's `refuse(reducedMotion, control)` with
  `reducedMotion()` read at that refusal and the refused Previous or Next button, in the kit
  `refuse`'s order. The two forms exclude each other.
- `PagerView.refuse(direction)` refuses a step for the game's key as a pointer refusal does, under
  `active()`, and does nothing without a refusal or after `dispose()`.
- With `cue` every refusal plays the kit cue unless it already plays on that step, and
  `stopRefusal()` and `dispose()` stop it, as in 0.82.0.
- With `refuse` the pager calls it on every pointer and key refusal and never calls the kit cue's
  `refuse`, `refusing` or `stopRefusal`. The game owns its cue's replay and stopping:
  `stopRefusal()` and `dispose()` leave it, and a kit cue the game plays itself, alone.
- Archie's Help passes its own `refuse(reducedMotion, element)` directly, as
  `refusal: { reducedMotion, refuse }`, and its Previous key refusal calls `pager.refuse(-1)`,
  which gives its red phases and whole-pixel step shake to both paths.
- Not covered: the slider keeps the kit cue only.
