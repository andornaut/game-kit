## Criteria

- Route Firefox toolbar Back through the same shared guard as Chromium.
- Limit the repair to the current owned marker and its exact base entry.
- Keep Back guarded after an ordinary first-install reload when GameKit can establish that its entry is at the end of history.
- Leave forward history intact when the browser cannot establish that the current entry is at the end of history.
- Preserve cancellation, one confirmed departure and unrelated history traversal.

## Outcome

The shared guard handles trusted untagged desktop Firefox Back on the owned entry pair.
A verified first-install reload can carry its prior history position, while a matching shape
with hidden forward history retains that entry. Real Firefox and Chromium tests covered
confirmation and departure. Physical Android remains unverified.
