## Criteria

- A HUD disc rim option draws an outer ring colour and width at the disc
  edge, transparent beyond the disc, in every state, so a disc with both a
  rim and an inset ring needs no art for its rings.
- The inset ring, fill and press geometry are unchanged; without a rim the
  disc renders as today.

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.88.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.88.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

Additive tokens; a disc that sets no rim token renders as before. No version bump.

- Rim: `--ui-hud-disc-rim`, base px wide (default 0), in `--ui-hud-disc-rim-colour` with
  `selected`, `focus`, `pressed` and `disabled` variants (`--ui-hud-disc-focus-rim-colour`), each
  left unset keeping the idle colour, as ring colours do. `--ui-hud-disc-rim-inset`, whole base px
  (default 0), puts the disc's edge that far inside the face's edge. The rim is the outer rim width
  of the band from the disc's edge to the ring, the state's fill covers the rest of the band, and
  nothing is painted beyond the disc's edge, so the corners and the margin outside the circle stay
  clear in every state. Both are spreads of the fill layer, not an outline. A ring inset below the
  rim inset plus the rim covers the rim. Disc, face, icon and press geometry do not move.
- Chibi's entry disc (35 unit frame, 33 unit disc): rim inset 1, a 1 unit ink rim, ring inset 2, a
  2 unit cream ring and the pose's navy or dslate fill, so its art keeps only the glyph. Exit is a
  standard button in Chibi, so it takes the same tokens once it is a HUD disc.
- Forced colours set the fill layer aside with its ring and rim, unchanged. A game that shows the
  layer there sets `forced-color-adjust: none` on it to keep the rim and band, which are box
  shadows.
- Focus harness: a rim disc samples clear outside the disc's edge and at the box corner and inside
  it outside the circle, the rim, the band's fill, the ring and the fill, at rest, focused and
  pressed, with each state's rim colour; forced colours hide its layer.
- Unchanged: main's and this change's stylesheets render 45 discs (five states; plain, painted and
  ring-inset; units 1, 1.5 and 4) and each focused painted disc to identical pixels, with the same
  boxes and the same layer inset, ring and fill. The computed shadow list only adds a transparent
  rim shadow.
