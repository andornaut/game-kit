## Criteria

- A HUD disc rim option draws an outer ring colour and width at the disc
  edge, transparent beyond the disc, in every state, so a disc with both a
  rim and an inset ring needs no art for its rings.
- The inset ring, fill and press geometry are unchanged; without a rim the
  disc renders as today.
