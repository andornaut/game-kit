## Criteria

- Placed form rows and sliders keep art-locked part sizes: the corner lands
  on the grid and the size is a whole number of art pixels, never stretched
  by snapping each edge separately.
- A placed list takes a gap per list and per row instead of a fixed stack gap.
- A one-action row variant hosts a single action whose press covers the row,
  or rows take a press margin per edge.
- Pressed rows can dip their art, as buttons do.
- Unplaced rows and today's defaults render as before.

## Outcome

Additive. Unplaced rows and a placed list without the new options render as in 0.89.0, which
`unprojected.test.ts` asserts by digest: of the stylesheets with projected and opt-in rules
removed, of the unplaced markup, and of a placed list's markup and geometry, the last taken
from 0.89.0's source.

- Art-locked sizes: `artPixel` on `formRowsGeometry`'s context, `sliderGeometry`'s context
  and `createFormRows` `place`. Icons, switches, the slider thumb, the track's and fill's
  height and the caps are their units times `artPixel` from their projected corner; rows,
  columns, the lane and the track's and fill's widths keep edge projection. An icon starts at its
  column's left edge and an end-placed state word's ink ends on the switch as drawn. Action groups
  take no art pixel: their faces are boxes.
- Row gaps: `FormRowLayout.gap` (whole units, `ACTION_GAPS.standard.stack` by default) for the
  unplaced and placed list, and `FormRowsRow.gap` or a `place` entry's `{ element, gap }` for
  the gap above one row, ignored on the first shown row.
- One-action rows: `createFormRow` `action: { size, className, role }` puts the label and
  optional icon on one standard button face centred in the row (`FormRowsRow.action`,
  `PlacedFormRow.face`); the row stays the button, so its press covers the row. Chosen over
  per-edge press margins, which would add a second hit area and clamps to a control that is
  already one press target.
- Press dip: `createFormRow` `pressDip` (whole units) moves every child but a one-action row's
  face down while pressed and not disabled; the face dips by `--ui-button-press-dip`. Placed, the
  dip is `pressDip` art pixels, or without `artPixel` the projected length from the row's top
  (`PlacedFormRow.pressDip`).
- Proved on Archie's grid at 360x608@2 (art pixel 2), 412x915@2.625 and 390x844@3 (art pixel
  3): at 3 the thumb is 30x48, the caps 12/12, the switch 96x48 and the icon 48x48 at every step
  and row; Settings rows 17 apart and adjacent Cheats switches 0 apart.
