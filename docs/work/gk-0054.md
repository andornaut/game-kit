## Criteria

- An action group owns spacing from the 16, 8 and 24 px tokens and pads or
  outsets children by the hit margin and half the gap, so consumers need no
  per-button padding or negative margins.
- The group lays out as a row when it fits and otherwise stacks, with the
  primary action last, and has a pinned footer variant with secondary at the
  left edge and primary at the right.
- Text-role classes, a native-button reset and a colour token mechanism games
  fill from their own palette.
- Retire or document `.ui-control-row` and `.ui-control-copy`.
- Reference implementations: Chibi `src/ui/dom/` (K4 to K6) and Ada ada-0390.
- Layout boxes equal the drawn faces. The press-area outset is clamped to half
  the gap, never overlaps a neighbour and never changes layout.
- Row-or-stack fitting measures actual label widths and never scales type.
  Hidden buttons do not count toward sole-button centring. A pager may sit
  between pinned footer actions.
- Lengths come from tokens or CSS variables the game sets, with no fixed px in
  component CSS. Colour tokens are role-named slots (card face, frame, text
  primary and secondary, accent, button face states) with forced-colours
  support and no per-game colour literals.
- One focus treatment per component. The native-button reset never overrides
  a game's component classes. Components take player text as input.
- Geometry is in game units with a game projection hook (Archie projects to
  integer device pixels), with no sub-pixel positions or percentage gaps.
- Face sizes come from the game: either measured label widths (Ada) or data
  sizes (Archie). Fitting decides from those sizes. Text placement takes the
  game's ink placement hook or is left to the game.
- Every computed position, size, gap and inset is a whole game unit. An odd
  pixel goes to one documented fixed side; never half a gap.
- Action-group tokens stay separate from layout spacing roles (row, group,
  panel padding and margin), and a group accepts a named gap override for
  exempt layouts.
- Art-locked sizes are laid out, never stretched or scaled. Buttons are placed
  by their face rectangle, with the lip inside the button height; gaps are
  measured between outer ink edges. Nine-slice insets come from art metadata.
- Fitting uses full font size only; a row that does not fit stacks.
- Focus, press and selection are fills only; forced colours use ButtonFace and
  Highlight fills. Disabled is distinct and does not dip.
