## Scope

Breaking 0.59.0 redesign: the page downloads and verifies the complete build under the one loading screen, resumably, with one determinate progress value; the service worker only routes to the active build; updates stage the delta in the background, switch silently at launch, and offer Update now through `onUpdateReady`; first-install and background-update state are separate; asset groups are removed. Design: [Install and update design](../install-and-update.md).

## Criteria

- A throttled Chromium install longer than 5 minutes completes, and a reload or worker stop resumes without repeat requests.
- A launch with a complete staged build switches silently with no requests; Update now saves, then switches with no requests; a failed background update never shows Loading failed.
- Old caches are reclaimed from the launching page.
- Each game has an adoption branch passing the consumer proof before release.

## Decisions

- Decisions (user, 2026-11-18): page installer with a router-only worker; defects 9 and 10 patched in 0.58.2 with a switch stopgap that skips re-hashing install-verified entries; asset groups removed in 0.59.0; kit packs plus Art sheets in 0.60.0; update discovery by a page fetch of `build.json`; Update now is game-rendered, the kit firing `onUpdateReady`; Archie boot loads every group behind the one loader once installed.
