## Criteria

- Capture one reproducible during-play workload per game through its existing Tech owner.
- Record frame-time percentiles and trace evidence, including the device and workload.
- Identify the first worthwhile optimization from each trace and any shared GameKit opportunity.

## Outcome

Each game supplied two matched Chromium 155 software WebGL gameplay runs and a separate
diagnostic trace. The samples measure animation callback cadence. The scenarios and canvas
sizes differ, and physical display performance remains unmeasured.

- Ada's busy battle had p95 intervals of 100.0 and 100.0 ms and p99 intervals of 100.1 and
  100.1 ms. Check whether hardware reproduces the synchronous layer readback waits.
- Archie's High Park walk had p95 intervals of 50.0 and 33.4 ms and p99 intervals of 50.1 and
  50.0 ms. Precompute static verge spans and require pixel parity before a matched replay.
- Chibi's Penthouse entrance had p95 intervals of 16.7 and 16.8 ms and p99 intervals of 16.8
  and 16.8 ms. Investigate hazard occupancy and movement-map search if a heavier workload
  reproduces a frame-time problem.

The traces identify separate costs. A shared Phaser renderer change has no measured benefit
from these captures. The coordination work record links the source pins and owner evidence.
