## Scope

Non-breaking 0.58.2 patch: write the complete-build selection only after verification (defect 9), expire the group activation lock at its deadline (defect 10), and on switch check presence of install-verified entries instead of re-hashing them (defect 8 stopgap). Design: [Install and update design](../install-and-update.md).

## Criteria

- A failed or killed activation keeps the previous selection, so navigations keep working.
- A replaced waiting worker's activating lock expires, so later installs proceed.
- A switch completes within `SWITCH_TIMEOUT_MS` on a single-CPU device without re-hashing install-verified entries.
- 0.58.2 is released with the gate, consumer proof, exact-main CI and tag CI green.

## Decisions

- Decisions (user, 2026-11-18): page installer with a router-only worker; defects 9 and 10 patched in 0.58.2 with a switch stopgap that skips re-hashing install-verified entries; asset groups removed in 0.59.0; kit packs plus Art sheets in 0.60.0; update discovery by a page fetch of `build.json`; Update now is game-rendered, the kit firing `onUpdateReady`; Archie boot loads every group behind the one loader once installed.

## Outcome

The selection is removed before checking and chosen on demand at navigation; an activating
barrier expires five minutes after its deadline; switch and activation check own entries by
presence. Released as 0.58.2.
