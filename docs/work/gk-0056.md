## Criteria

- A switch and labelled form row with a trailing value stack, its track sized
  by CSS.
- A pager (previous, count, next) with unavailable and refusal states.
- A round HUD disc with idle, focus, pressed and disabled faces from tokens.
- Reference implementations: Chibi `toggleValue.ts`, `settings.css`,
  `achievements.ts` and `stageSelect.ts` (K2, K3) and Ada ada-0390 item 2.
- The switch track is sized by a CSS grid column, with the visible value
  centred under the switch and the row gap from the stack token.
- The pager uses one gap token.
- HUD discs keep fixed disc and hit geometry across states, change only fill
  or icon colour (no rings), accept the game's SVG with side-bearing
  compensation and take a size token per instance.
- The switch's state word placement, row height and icon slot come from the
  game. The HUD disc takes the game's art and press treatment. Focus and press
  are fills only.
- The whole form row is the target, and the row icon is one state-free frame.
- Pager Previous and Next are standard buttons; dots are decorative and not
  focusable.
- An action group can keep each action's full minimum press area, overlapping
  its neighbours', and route a press to the nearest face centre, the earlier
  action on a tie; the clamped default is unchanged (Archie Art, press.min).

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.77.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.77.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

- `runtime/ui/formRow` `formRowPresentation(layout)` and
  `runtime/web/ui/dom/formRow` `createFormRows` and `createFormRow` with
  `formRow.css`: a list whose grid columns every row shares as a subgrid, so
  the value column is the widest value stack or a fixed `track`, sized by CSS.
  Each row is one standard button: optional state-free icon, label (its name),
  note (its description) and trailing value stack. A switch row is
  `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`, the game's `ui-switch` frames at their
  art-locked size and the state word below, centred or with its ink's right
  edge on the switch's by the word's bearing. Row height, padding, column gap,
  icon slot and offsets, switch size and value gap come from the game; rows are
  the stacked action gap apart, with a clear idle face and no dip in the theme.
- `runtime/ui/pager` `arrangePager`, `pagerSize`, `pagerState` and `PAGER_GAP`,
  and `runtime/web/ui/dom/pager` `createPager` with `pager.css`: Previous, the
  count and Next as an action group with one gap token, the buttons standard
  buttons in the game's appearance, unavailable steps disabled or pointer
  refusals with `refusalCue`, the count as text or decorative dots that take no
  focus or presses, sized to be a footer's `middle` slot.
- `runtime/ui/hudDisc` `hudDiscIconOffset` and `hudDiscGeometry`, and
  `runtime/web/ui/dom/hudDisc` `createHudDisc` with `hudDisc.css`: a round
  standard button whose box is its face at a size token per instance, placed
  and given its press area by an action group; state changes only the face fill
  or image and the icon colour; the game's face art, art face images per state
  and press dip; the icon's ink centred by its side bearings, odd unit right and
  below.
- `runtime/ui/actionGroup` `pressOverlap: "nearest"` and
  `pressedAction(arrangement, point)` (on `hitAreas` `pressedArea`): full press
  areas that may overlap, nearest-centre routing with ties to the earlier
  action, and outsets cut at the whole-unit nearest edge so the DOM group's hit
  testing routes the same way.
- Not covered: form rows are laid out by CSS, not projected; a centred switch
  is on whole units only when its value column's right edge is. Sliders stay on
  their own continuous path.
