## Criteria

- Recipe-based delivery emits the versioned recipe and omits the redundant
  raw group artifacts. The manifest, pointer, archive URLs and bytes that
  the assembler serves stay unchanged.
- Grouped staging requires group support from the controlling worker.
  Keep current first install, specific-file updates and transactional
  activation available.
- Games qualify their current asset inventory and retire predecessor-only
  copies through their normal delivery.
