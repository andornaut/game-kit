## Criteria

- Include `.txt` entries with their original URLs and bytes, served as UTF-8 plain text.
- Preserve first-install packs and rejection of unknown asset types.

## Outcome

The archive producer records `.txt` as `text/plain; charset=utf-8`.
The archive fixture checks the media type and verifies the original bytes,
including non-ASCII text and CRLF line endings.
