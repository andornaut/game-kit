## Criteria

- A grid action group: rows of columns with one gap rule, for two-column
  cards.
- Press clamping between neighbouring groups and between a group and a form
  row, so games need no z-order or inset rules.
- Game-defined text role names beyond the kit's four.
- Pager refusal does not restart a cue that is already playing.
- HUD disc face fills can draw a ring and a distinct focus fill without
  per-state art.
- Button frame-state rules usable on faces other than the 48 px label face.
- A lone action stays centred (user decision); no start-aligned option.

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.82.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.82.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

All additions are opt-in. Callers that use none of them get the same geometry,
properties and attributes, with one exception: a pager refusal no longer
restarts a cue that is already playing, and `dispose()` stops it. No released
consumer uses the pager. Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

- Grid: `runtime/ui/actionGroup` `arrangeActionGrid(context, items)` and the
  DOM group's `arrangeGrid`. Rows of at most `columns`, with cells at the
  widest face or the room split evenly (`cellWidth`) and `locked` faces centred
  in their cells. A short last row is centred and an oversized grid takes fewer
  columns. Press areas clamp toward every neighbour along and between rows,
  with nearest overlap supported. Chibi's Pause becomes one two-column `fill`
  grid with Resume `locked`: the 4 unit press margin between rows falls out of
  the share rule.
- Press clamping: `separatePressAreas(members)` applies the in-group share rule
  between members (arrangements in one space, and boxes that are their own press
  target). Diagonal neighbours split along the axis with more space, the stacked
  axis on a tie. Faces that overlap, such as scrolled content under a footer,
  stay as they are. `runtime/web/ui/dom/actionGroup` `createPressContext(root)`
  measures members in whole base px, writes clamped outsets and reapplies on a
  member's arrange. A view that wraps a group, like a modal frame's `footer`, is
  that group. Cheats' z-order rule and Stage select's faces-to-pager inset are
  no longer needed. `pressOverlap: "nearest"` keeps routing inside its group.
- Text roles: `setGameTextRoles(roles, root)` takes lower camelCase game names,
  and `setTextRole(role, element)` adds the `ui-text-role` class with
  references to the role's lengths, so Chibi's five roles need no game CSS.
  Kit role names are refused.
- Pager: `refusalCue` `refusing(element)` and `stopRefusal(element)`. The pager
  plays the cue only when none is playing on that step, and `stopRefusal()`
  lets the scene stop it.
- HUD disc: `hudDisc.css` adds a fill layer inside the edge and under the art
  and icon, built from `--ui-hud-disc-fill`, a `--ui-hud-disc-ring` width with
  `--ui-hud-disc-ring-colour`, and `--ui-hud-disc-ink`. Each has selected,
  focus, pressed and disabled variants. Unset, a state shows the theme's fill.
  Forced colours set the layer aside. Chibi's entry is edge 1 (frame slot), a
  2-unit cream ring and a navy or dslate fill; the skill disc's cream focus is
  `--ui-hud-disc-focus-fill`, so art no longer paints every state.
- Button frames: the new `buttonFrame.css` with `ui-button-frame` and
  `ui-button-frame-content`. A nine-slice frame of any size follows its button's
  primary, selected, pressed, primary-pressed and disabled tokens. Press and
  focus fill its inside from the pressed slot and the one focus fill, content
  shifts by tokens, and forced colours use system edges and fills. Chibi's stage
  faces need the kit's `data-ui-pressed` and `data-ui-button-role` in place of
  their own attributes.
- A lone action stays centred in groups and grids, with no start-aligned
  option.
- Not covered: the press context measures drawn boxes, so a layout applied
  while a refusal cue shakes a form row is off by the shake until the next
  apply. HUD disc glyphs drawn as images still need the game's mask to take
  `--ui-hud-disc-ink`.
