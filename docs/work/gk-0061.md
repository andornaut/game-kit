## Criteria

- A non-modal full-screen page variant: covered chrome such as a top bar stays
  live, with no dialog isolation.
- Hooks for the game's ink-placed title and its footer (such as a page Back),
  with full-screen insets from the game.
- Placement on the game's device pixel grid through the projection hook, as
  the pure geometry already supports.
- A body whose scrolling the game may drive instead of native scroll.
- Archie's Pet shop, Logbook and Help pages adopt it pixel-identically.
- From Chibi's adoption: a modal frame with no title, a backdrop that can
  cover more than the frame's content rect (letterbox bars), and a footer that
  can be measured before its first layout.

## Outcome

Published in [GameKit 0.81.0](https://github.com/andornaut/game-kit/releases/tag/v0.81.0).
Consumer adoption follows each game's normal delivery.

All additions are opt-in; a frame that uses none of them writes the same
properties and attributes as before. Consumer adoption follows each game's
normal delivery.

- `runtime/ui/modalFrame`: `variant: "page"` places as full screen;
  `insets: { side, bottom }` replace the padding for the body content and
  footer; `sizes.title` is optional, the untitled header being the padding or
  the close clearance; `context.backdrop` returns a projected `backdrop` rect.
- `runtime/web/ui/dom/modalFrame`: a page hides its backdrop and opens without
  the modal layer, dialog isolation, `aria-modal` or inert marks, so a top bar
  stays live. An untitled frame takes `label` as its `aria-label`. With
  `project`, every length is whole CSS px on the game's grid and the side and
  bottom insets are projected; the game supplies the title and body heights.
  `parts.title.height` and `parts.footer: { height }` with `footerSlot` let the
  game own its title ink and footer, placed from the returned `units` layout.
  `parts.scroll: "game"` leaves scrolling, clipping and pans to the game.
  `footer.measure()` shows the footer slot while it measures.
- Archie's Pet shop, Logbook and Help geometry is proved in the pure test:
  content under the 40 top bar, padding 16, title 15 with inset 24 (header 63,
  title row at 16, 64, 328 wide), insets side 8 and bottom 12, footer 48. The
  body is 103 to 492, Back at 116, 500 for any label width, and every rect
  lands on Archie's device grid at four scale and DPR pairs.
- Chibi's frame items 4 to 6 are covered by the untitled label, the backdrop
  rect and the footer measure.
- No action group change was needed: the projected page uses a game-owned
  footer, since the DOM action group places at `--ui-unit` only.
